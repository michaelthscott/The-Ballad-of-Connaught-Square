# The Ballad of Connaught Square

## Ballad

### Title

> The rest after the ballad's title appears, as the performance starts, before the introduction.

- Rest: 0s–2s

### Introduction

> Plays once, before the ballad, in place of the first canto's intermission.

- Violin: 2s–11s
- Viola: 1s–12s
- Cello: 0s–13s
- Rest: 13s–15s

## Canto

### Title

> The rest after each canto's title appears, once any intermission before it has finished, before its first stanza's music.

- Rest: 0s–2s

### Intermission

> Plays after each canto, before the next, in place of the stanza intermission. Nothing plays after the last canto.

- Violin, Viola, Cello: 0s–11s
- Rest: 11s–12s

## Stanza

### First line

> Opens the first part of the first line of each stanza, straight after an intermission.

- Violin, Cello: 0s–1s
- Viola: 0s–1s, 1s–end+1s at 90%
- Recite: 1s

### Later line

> Opens the first part of each later line of a stanza.

- Violin, Cello: 0s–3s
- Viola: 0s–3s, 3s–end+1s at 90%
- Recite: 3s

### Intermission

> Plays after each stanza, before the next in the same canto.

- Violin, Viola, Cello: 0s–5s
- Rest: 5s–7s

## Line

### Later parts

> Opens each part of a line after its first.

- Viola continues: 0s–end at 90%
- Recite: 0.25s
